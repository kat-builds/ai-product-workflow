# Legal Boundary Notes

Use these rules when a logo request includes legal language.

## Must State Clearly

- Do not promise trademark registration success.
- Do not promise global uniqueness.
- Do not claim formal trademark clearance.
- Do not provide legal advice.
- Do provide practical risk-reduction design decisions.

## Safe Wording Example

"This mark is designed as original artwork for the project and intentionally avoids obvious similarity to major known marks. Formal trademark clearance requires legal review."

## Workflow Guidance

- Deliver design assets normally.
- Add a concise legal boundary note when legal certainty is relevant.
- If the user requests filing certainty or trademark clearance, recommend qualified legal review.
